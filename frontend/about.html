Our History

From One Route to the World

What started as a small Mozambican virtual airline has evolved into a powerhouse of African virtual aviation. Our journey reflects the dynamism and ambition of the African continent itself.

1 March 2020
Africana Virtual Airways is Founded

AFV takes to the virtual skies with our inaugural flight from Maputo (FQMA) to Nacala (FQNC), operated by a small Cessna 172. A historic moment for Sub-Saharan VATSIM aviation.

Late 2020
Maputo Hub Expansion

The success of our inaugural route led to rapid expansion. We launched 12 new destinations across Mozambique, Southern Africa and East Africa, adding the Boeing 737-800 to our fleet.

2021
First Intercontinental Route

Africana spread its wings beyond Africa with the launch of Maputo–Dubai, flown by a Boeing 787-10. This established AFV as a true intercontinental operator.

2022
Algiers Hub Opens

To better serve North Africa and provide a European gateway, Africana opened its second hub at Houari Boumediene Airport (DAAG) in Algiers, Algeria. The A340-600 joined the fleet for European services.

2023
A380 Enters Service

C9-AJA, our Airbus A380, made AFV the first Sub-Saharan virtual airline to operate the superjumbo. The double-decker giant now connects Maputo to the world's busiest routes.

2024
Today: 38 Routes and Growing

Africana Airways now operates 38 routes, 17 aircraft, and serves over 60 active pilots. We remain committed to growing African aviation and welcoming new members.

Who We Are

Aviation Built for Africa

African-First Network

We are the only virtual airline whose primary hub is rooted in Mozambique and the East African coast, serving routes that real-world carriers have long overlooked.

Our Mission

To celebrate and grow African aviation by building a welcoming, realistic, and ambitious virtual airline that reflects the energy, diversity, and potential of the continent.

A Community, Not Just a Roster

Africana Airways is built around its pilots. From your first flight out of Maputo to long-haul intercontinental operations, every member is part of the story we are writing together.

Realistic Operations

From dynamic fuel-adjusted pricing to accurate great-circle routes and live VATSIM traffic, every detail is designed to deliver an authentic airline experience in the virtual skies.

VATSIM Integration

Flying the AFV Network on VATSIM

All AFV flights are operated on the VATSIM network. Here's what you need to know.

Callsign Prefix

All Africana flights use the AFV callsign prefix (e.g. AFV101, AFV205).

Division

We operate primarily within the VATSSA (VATSIM Sub-Saharan Africa) division.

Flight Plans

Use our SimBrief OFP integration and file your VATSIM flight plan directly from the dispatch portal.

Rank System

Accumulate flight hours to progress through AFV ranks from Junior Officer to Chief Pilot.

Events

Regular group flights, Africa Cross-Continent events and formation flying across Mozambique and Algeria.

How to Join

Create a VATSIM account, register on our platform, and file your first flight plan. No minimum hours required.
